# Template 8

## Register
Product for the dealer workspace; brand for the public showroom.

## Users and purpose
Customers browse vehicles, compare finance estimates and contact the dealership. Existing DMS staff manage their dealership stock through narrow website APIs. Preserve the existing public template and its contact, finance and part exchange journeys.

## Design direction
The existing public code establishes a dark luxury showroom with red accents, Montserrat typography and restrained uppercase navigation. The workspace serves staff in a normal daylight office; use readable light surfaces with red actions and the existing typeface. Keep staff styles isolated.

## Constraints
Use the supplied modernization and Light DMS guides. Avoid copying another dealer identity or replacing public content with template 2 branding. Customer flows must distinguish enquiries, pending delivery and confirmed success. No online payments. Preserve accessible labels, keyboard controls, focus and mobile usability.
